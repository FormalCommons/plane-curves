## Notation

Everything is in the namespace `PlaneCurves.Projective`, over a field `k`,
with `[IsAlgClosed k]` only where stated. `F : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) k` is a
form of degree `n` (`F.IsHomogeneous n`). `X 0, X 1, X 2` are Fulton's
`X, Y, Z`.

- `fundamentalPoint i := mk k (Pi.single i 1) _`. Fulton's `P = [0:0:1]` is
  `fundamentalPoint 2`, `P' = [0:1:0]` is `fundamentalPoint 1` and
  `P'' = [1:0:0]` is `fundamentalPoint 0`. The exceptional line opposite
  `fundamentalPoint i` is `V(X i)`. These are the conventions of
  [`design/T3.md`](T3.md#notation).
- `r i := (mult F (fundamentalPoint i)).toNat`. Fulton's `r, r', r''` are
  `r 2, r 1, r 0`.
- "`F` has no exceptional component" means `∀ i, ¬ X i ∣ F`. For an
  irreducible `F` this is Fulton's "`F` is not an exceptional line". It
  implies `F ≠ 0`.
- `mult`, `leadingForm`, `IsTangentAt` are Q1 and Q3's
  (`Projective/Multiplicity.lean`, `Projective/Tangent.lean`). `zeroLocus` and
  `vanishingIdeal` are `ProjectiveSpace.zeroLocus` and
  `ProjectiveSpace.vanishingIdeal` (P4). `dehomogenize` is
  `TauCeti.dehomogenize`.

## The mathematics, and the route

All of (1)–(4) is polynomial algebra. It rests on three identities.

**The expansion at `Z`.** Write `F = ∑_{j ≤ n} G_j(X, Y) Z^{n-j}` with
`G_j := homogeneousComponent j (dehomogenize 2 F)`. This is
`MvPolynomial.homogenize_dehomogenize` (`RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Homogenize/Basic.lean`)
unfolded with `homogenize_def`. Then `r 2` is the least `j` with
`G_j ≠ 0` (`mult_eq_orderAt_dehomogenize` and L1's
`le_orderAt_iff_homogeneousComponent`), and `G_{r 2}` is the leading form
`leadingFormAt (dehomogenize 2 F) 0`. Substituting gives

```
F^Q = ∑_j Z^j · G_j(Y, X) · (XY)^{n-j}.
```

So `Z^{r 2}` divides `F^Q`. Modulo `Z`, `F^Q / Z^{r 2}` is
`G_{r 2}(Y, X) (XY)^{n - r 2} ≠ 0`, so no higher power of `Z` divides `F^Q`.
This is (1) at `Z`, for **every nonzero form**, with no hypothesis on
exceptional components. For example, `F = X` gives `F^Q = YZ`, with `r 2 = r 1 = 1`.

**The involution.** `(F^Q)^Q = (XYZ)^n F`, because
`F(XZ·XY, YZ·XY, YZ·XZ) = F(XYZ·X, XYZ·Y, XYZ·Z)` and `F` is homogeneous of
degree `n`. With (1) this gives, in the domain `k[X, Y, Z]`,

```
X^{r 1 + r 2} Y^{r 0 + r 2} Z^{r 0 + r 1} · (F')^Q = (XYZ)^n F.
```

Now assume `F` has no exceptional component, and compare the powers of `X`.
The left side contains `X^{r 1 + r 2 + r 0(F')}`, by (1) for `F'`; the right
side contains exactly `X^n`. So `m_{P''}(F') = n − r 1 − r 2`, and likewise
for `P'` and `P`. That is (3). Cancelling, `(F')^Q = X^{n−r1−r2} Y^{n−r0−r2} Z^{n−r0−r1} F`,
so `(F')' = F`. Both need the hypothesis. For `F = X` we get `F' = 1`,
`(F')' = 1 ≠ F`, and `m_{P''}(F') = 0 ≠ 1 − 1 − 1`.

**Multiplicativity.** `F ↦ F^Q` is a ring map, and `r i` is additive (Q1's
`mult_mul`). So `(GH)' = G'H'`. Irreducibility follows. Let `F` be
irreducible with no exceptional component, and suppose `F' = GH`. Then `G`
and `H` have no exceptional component, and `F = (F')' = G'H'`. So `G'` or
`H'` is a unit, say `G'`. Then `G = (G')'` is a unit too.

**Symmetry.** `quadraticTransform` commutes with every permutation of the
variables: `(rename σ F)^Q = rename σ (F^Q)`. A renaming moves
`fundamentalPoint i` to `fundamentalPoint (σ i)` with the same
multiplicity. So `properTransform (rename σ F) = rename σ (properTransform F)`,
and good position is invariant too. Every statement about one fundamental
point or one exceptional line is **proved once**, at the index the proof
finds easiest:

- statements about `Z`, the point `P = fundamentalPoint 2` and the expansion
  above are proved at `2`;
- the tangency criterion is proved at `P' = fundamentalPoint 1` and the line
  `Z`, where Q3's Problem 5.5 (`mult_eq_and_isTangentAt_iff_of_eq_sum`) is
  stated.

The general statement follows with `σ = Equiv.swap i 2` (or the permutation
that moves the right pair of indices). Each general statement is proved by a
short `rename` argument, never by repeating the proof.

**Good position (4).** `IsGoodPosition F` says that no exceptional line is
tangent to `F` at a fundamental point. An exceptional component would be
tangent at the two fundamental points it passes through, so good position
implies that `F` has no exceptional component (and `F ≠ 0`). The criterion
for a coordinate line is this. For `F` not divisible by `Z`, the
restriction `F(X, Y, 0)` has `X`-adic order at least `m_{P'}(F)`, with
equality iff `Z` is not tangent at `P'`. In Problem 5.5's form
`F = ∑ G_i(X, Z) Y^{n−i}`, that order is the least `i` with `Z ∤ G_i`. The
restriction of `F'` to `Z = 0` is

```
X^{r 0} Y^{r 1} · F'(X, Y, 0) = (XY)^{n − r 2} · G_{r 2}(Y, X)
```

by the expansion. So `Z` is tangent to `F'` at `P'` iff `Y ∣ G_{r 2}`, that
is, iff `Y` is tangent to `F` at `P`. This is Fulton's argument without
intersection numbers. The other five pairs (point, line) follow by symmetry.

**The geometric half of (2).** Fix it as follows.

- The point map `quadraticMap` sends `[x:y:z]` to `[yz:xz:xy]` off the
  fundamental points. On the complement `U` of `fundamentalTriangle` it is
  an involution. Let `v = (x, y, z)` have no zero coordinate, and let
  `w = (yz, xz, xy)`. Then `w_0^{r 0} w_1^{r 1} w_2^{r 2} · eval w F' = eval w F^Q = (xyz)^n · eval v F`,
  and the monomial factor is nonzero. So
  `quadraticMap` is a bijection from `V(F) ∖ triangle` onto
  `V(F') ∖ triangle`, for every form `F`. It maps every point of `V(F')`
  that is not fundamental into `V(F)`. It maps `L ∖ {P', P''}` to `P`.
- **Closure.** The library has no Zariski topology on `ℙ²`. The closure of a
  set `S` is `zeroLocus (vanishingIdeal S)`, the smallest algebraic set
  containing it (P4's `subset_zeroLocus_vanishingIdeal` and
  `zeroLocus_vanishingIdeal_zeroLocus`). For `[IsAlgClosed k]`, `F`
  irreducible with no exceptional component:
  `zeroLocus (vanishingIdeal (quadraticMap '' (zeroLocus {F} \ fundamentalTriangle))) = zeroLocus {F'}`.
  `⊆` holds because the image is `V(F') ∖ triangle ⊆ V(F')`. For `⊇`, let `G`
  be homogeneous and vanish on `V(F') ∖ triangle`. Then `XYZ·G` vanishes on
  `V(F')`, so `F' ∣ XYZ·G` by P7's `vanishingIdeal_zeroLocus_singleton`,
  since `F'` is irreducible. `F'` is prime and divides none of `X, Y, Z`,
  so `F' ∣ G`.
- **Birationality** is an isomorphism of function fields, the algebraic
  meaning of "`Q` restricts to a birational morphism `C' ⇢ C`":
  `quadraticFunctionFieldEquiv : functionField F ≃ₐ[k] functionField F'`,
  with `G/H ↦ G^Q/H^Q`. It is well defined and lands in `k(C')` because, for
  every `H`, `F ∣ H ↔ F' ∣ H^Q`. For `→`, `F' ∣ F^Q ∣ H^Q`. For `←`, apply
  `Q` again: `(F')^Q ∣ (XYZ)^d H`, and `F ∣ (F')^Q`, so `F ∣ (XYZ)^d H`, and
  `F ∣ H` since `F` is prime and divides no `X i`. The same map for `F'`
  goes back to `k(C)`, because `(F')' = F`. The two composites are the
  identity, since `G^{QQ}/H^{QQ} = (XYZ)^d G / ((XYZ)^d H)`. A set-level
  bijection alone would not say "birational": Frobenius is a bijection.
  `functionField F` is Q5's (`ProjectiveSpace.functionField` of the ideal
  `(F)`), so this one piece needs Q5.

## Definitions and their API

### Fundamental points and the triangle (`Basic.lean`)

These are the definitions and API that `design/T3.md` lists "unless T2 has
them". T2 has them, so T3 adds nothing here.

- `fundamentalPoint (i : Fin 3) : ℙ k (Fin 3 → k)`, `mk k (Pi.single i 1) _`.
  API: `fundamentalPoint_def`, `fundamentalPoint_injective`,
  `fundamentalPoint_mem_zeroLocus_X_iff`
  (`fundamentalPoint i ∈ zeroLocus {X j} ↔ i ≠ j`),
  `fundamentalPoint_mem_affineOpen_iff` (`↔ i = j`, for P1's `affineOpen j`),
  `affineChart_fundamentalPoint` (`affineChart i (fundamentalPoint i) = 0`),
  `mult_fundamentalPoint_eq_orderAt` (`F.IsHomogeneous n →
  mult F (fundamentalPoint i) = (dehomogenize i F).orderAt 0`), and
  `mult_rename_fundamentalPoint`
  (`mult (rename σ F) (fundamentalPoint (σ i)) = mult F (fundamentalPoint i)`,
  from `mult_rename` in Q1's file).
- `fundamentalTriangle : Set (ℙ k (Fin 3 → k))`, `zeroLocus {X 0 * X 1 * X 2}`.
  API: `fundamentalTriangle_def`, `mem_fundamentalTriangle_iff`
  (`∃ i, P.rep i = 0`), `mk_mem_fundamentalTriangle_iff`,
  `fundamentalPoint_mem_fundamentalTriangle`,
  `fundamentalTriangle_eq_iUnion` (`= ⋃ i, zeroLocus {X i}`).

### The algebraic transform (`Basic.lean`)

- `quadraticTransform : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R →ₐ[R] MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R`,
  `bind₁ ![X 1 * X 2, X 0 * X 2, X 0 * X 1]`, over any `[CommSemiring R]`.
  It is an `AlgHom`, so `map_mul`, `map_pow`, `map_prod` and `map_smul` come
  for free, and `quadraticTransform F` reads as Fulton's `F^Q`. API:
  - `quadraticTransform_def`; `quadraticTransform_X` (`= ∏ j ∈ {i}ᶜ, X j`)
    and the three `@[simp]` instances `quadraticTransform_X_zero` etc.;
    `quadraticTransform_C`;
  - `eval_quadraticTransform` (`eval v F^Q = eval ![v 1 * v 2, v 0 * v 2, v 0 * v 1] F`);
  - `IsHomogeneous.quadraticTransform` (degree `2n`, from Mathlib's
    `MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.aeval`); `quadraticTransform_eq_zero_iff`
    (`F^Q = 0 ↔ F = 0`, over a domain; injectivity from the involution, or
    by `bind₁` of algebraically independent monomials);
  - `quadraticTransform_quadraticTransform`
    (`F.IsHomogeneous n → F^{QQ} = (X 0 * X 1 * X 2) ^ n * F`, by
    `IsHomogeneous.aeval_smul`-style scaling, or monomial by monomial);
  - `quadraticTransform_rename` (`σ : Equiv.Perm (Fin 3)`:
    `quadraticTransform (rename σ F) = rename σ (quadraticTransform F)`, by
    `algHom_ext`).
- `quadraticTransform_eq_sum` (the expansion, for `F.IsHomogeneous n`):
  `F^Q = ∑ j ∈ range (n + 1), X 2 ^ j * rename ![1, 0] (homogeneousComponent j (dehomogenize 2 F)) * (X 0 * X 1) ^ (n - j)`.
  `rename ![1, 0]` sends the two variables of the binary form to `Y`, `X`,
  Fulton's `G_j(Y, X)`. This is the form T3 uses as well.

### The proper transform (`Basic.lean`)

- `properTransform (F : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) k) : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) k`,
  `quadraticTransform F /ᵐ exceptionalExponent F`, with Mathlib's
  `MvPolynomial.divMonomial` (`Algebra/MvPolynomial/Division.lean`) and
  `exceptionalExponent F : Fin 3 →₀ ℕ := Finsupp.equivFunOnFinite.symm fun i ↦ (mult F (fundamentalPoint i)).toNat`.
  The exponent of `X i` is the multiplicity at the fundamental point *off*
  `V(X i)`. So the definition is symmetric, and needs no case split. Junk
  values: `properTransform 0 = 0` (`mult 0 = ⊤`, whose `toNat` is `0`), and
  for a non-homogeneous `F` the value is not meaningful. Every theorem
  assumes `F` homogeneous. This is `DESIGN.md`'s choice, dividing by the
  powers that the multiplicities give. The alternative, dividing by the
  largest powers of `X, Y, Z` that divide `F^Q`, is the same polynomial by
  (1) (`multiplicity_X_quadraticTransform`), so it needs no definition of
  its own. API:
  - `properTransform_def`, `exceptionalExponent_def`,
    `exceptionalExponent_apply`;
  - `quadraticTransform_eq_monomial_mul_properTransform`
    (`F^Q = monomial (exceptionalExponent F) 1 * F'`), proved from (1) by
    `divMonomial_monomial_mul`; and Fulton's form `quadraticTransform_eq`
    (`F^Q = X 0 ^ r 0 * X 1 ^ r 1 * X 2 ^ r 2 * F'`);
  - `multiplicity_X_quadraticTransform` (`F ≠ 0`:
    `multiplicity (X i) (F^Q) = (mult F (fundamentalPoint i)).toNat`) and
    `not_X_dvd_properTransform` (`F ≠ 0 → ¬ X i ∣ F'`), the two halves of (1);
  - `IsHomogeneous.properTransform`
    (degree `2 * n - ∑ i, (mult F (fundamentalPoint i)).toNat`), and
    `properTransform_eq_zero_iff` (`F' = 0 ↔ F = 0`);
  - multiplicativity: `properTransform_mul` (`F`, `G` homogeneous),
    `properTransform_pow`, `properTransform_prod`, `properTransform_smul`
    (`c ≠ 0`), `properTransform_C` (`c ≠ 0`: `= C c`),
    `properTransform_X` (`= 1`), `properTransform_one`;
  - symmetry: `exceptionalExponent_rename`, `properTransform_rename`;
  - the restriction to `L`:
    `aeval_X_two_eq_zero_properTransform` (`F.IsHomogeneous n`, `F ≠ 0`):
    `X 0 ^ r 0 * X 1 ^ r 1 * aeval ![X 0, X 1, 0] F' = (X 0 * X 1) ^ (n - r 2) * rename ![1, 0] (leadingFormAt (dehomogenize 2 F) 0)`.
    T3's `aeval_X2_eq_zero_properTransform` is this identity with
    `r 0 = r 1 = 0`. T3 may state it as a one-line corollary, or use this
    form directly.
  - Example: the conic `XY + XZ + YZ` passes through the three fundamental
    points. Its proper transform is the line `X + Y + Z`, and the proper
    transform of that line is the conic again.
- `dvd_quadraticTransform_iff` (`F` irreducible with no exceptional
  component, any `H`): `F' ∣ H^Q ↔ F ∣ H`. Piece T2.6 needs it, and it is
  natural API next to irreducibility.

### Good position (`Basic.lean`)

- `IsGoodPosition (F : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) k) : Prop`,
  `∀ i j, i ≠ j → ¬ IsTangentAt F (fundamentalPoint i) (X j)`: no
  exceptional line is tangent to `C` at a fundamental point (Fulton p. 88).
  The fundamental points on `V(X j)` are those with `i ≠ j`. A point not on
  `C` has no tangent, since its leading form is a nonzero constant. API:
  - `isGoodPosition_def`; `IsGoodPosition.smul` and
    `isGoodPosition_smul_iff` (`c ≠ 0`); `isGoodPosition_rename_iff`;
  - `IsGoodPosition.ne_zero`, `IsGoodPosition.not_X_dvd` (no exceptional
    component, since `X j ∣ F` makes `X j` divide the leading form at each
    fundamental point on it), so (2)–(3) apply;
  - the criterion for coordinate lines, at `P'` and `Z` (Problem 5.5's
    point), for `F.IsHomogeneous n` and `¬ X 2 ∣ F`:
    `mult_fundamentalPoint_one_le_multiplicity` (`(mult F (fundamentalPoint 1)).toNat ≤ multiplicity (X 0) (aeval ![X 0, X 1, 0] F)`)
    and `isTangentAt_fundamentalPoint_one_X_two_iff`
    (`IsTangentAt F (fundamentalPoint 1) (X 2) ↔ (mult F (fundamentalPoint 1)).toNat < multiplicity (X 0) (aeval ![X 0, X 1, 0] F)`).
    The general form `isTangentAt_fundamentalPoint_X_iff` is for `i`, `j`, `l`
    pairwise distinct, with the restriction `bind₁ (Function.update X j 0) F`
    (the substitution of `Algebra/MvPolynomial/SetVarZero.lean`) and
    `multiplicity (X l)`. It follows by symmetry;
  - `isTangentAt_fundamentalPoint_two_iff_dvd_leadingFormAt`: for `i = 0, 1`,
    `X i` is tangent to `F` at `P` iff the image of `X i` divides
    `leadingFormAt (dehomogenize 2 F) 0`. This is Q3's
    `isTangentAt_iff_affine` in the chart `Z = 1`.
  - Examples: a line through no fundamental point is in good position, and a
    nonzero `F` with an exceptional component is not.

### The function field (`Birational.lean`)

- `quadraticFunctionFieldHom (F G : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) k) [Fact (Irreducible F)] [Fact (Irreducible G)] (hF : F.IsHomogeneous n) (hG : G.IsHomogeneous m) (h : ∀ H, G ∣ quadraticTransform H ↔ F ∣ H) : functionField F →ₐ[k] functionField G`.
  It sends `functionField.mk G₁ H₁` (P11b's `functionField.mk`, forms of
  equal degree `d`) to `functionField.mk G₁^Q H₁^Q` (degree `2d`). It is
  well defined by `functionField.mk_eq_mk_iff` and `h`. The construction may
  instead restrict the map of fraction fields of the homogeneous coordinate
  rings, induced by `Ideal.quotientMap` and `IsLocalization.map`, to degree
  zero, if that is shorter. API: `quadraticFunctionFieldHom_mk`.
- `quadraticFunctionFieldEquiv (F) [Fact (Irreducible F)] [Fact (Irreducible (properTransform F))] (hF : F.IsHomogeneous n) (hX : ∀ i, ¬ X i ∣ F) : functionField F ≃ₐ[k] functionField (properTransform F)`,
  built by `AlgEquiv.ofAlgHom` from the homs for `(F, F')` and `(F', F)`.
  The second is valid because `(F')' = F`, so `dvd_quadraticTransform_iff`
  for `F'` is its hypothesis `h`. API: `quadraticFunctionFieldEquiv_mk`,
  `quadraticFunctionFieldEquiv_symm_mk`.
  The second `Fact` is an instance argument, so that `functionField F'`
  elaborates. Callers supply it with
  `haveI := Fact.mk (irreducible_properTransform hF hirr hX)`.

## Main results

### (1)–(4), polynomial algebra (`Basic.lean`)

For `F.IsHomogeneous n`:

1. `multiplicity_X_quadraticTransform`, `not_X_dvd_properTransform`,
   `quadraticTransform_eq` (`F ≠ 0`).
2. `IsHomogeneous.properTransform`, and, with `hX : ∀ i, ¬ X i ∣ F`:
   - `quadraticTransform_properTransform`:
     `(F')^Q = X 0 ^ (n - r 1 - r 2) * X 1 ^ (n - r 0 - r 2) * X 2 ^ (n - r 0 - r 1) * F`;
   - `properTransform_properTransform`: `properTransform F' = F`;
   - `irreducible_properTransform` (`Irreducible F` as well);
   - `dvd_quadraticTransform_iff` (`Irreducible F` as well).
3. `mult_properTransform_fundamentalPoint_add` (`hX`; `i, j, l` pairwise
   distinct): `mult F' (fundamentalPoint i) + mult F (fundamentalPoint j) + mult F (fundamentalPoint l) = n`,
   in `ℕ∞`, with no truncated subtraction. There are also Fulton's three forms
   `mult_properTransform_fundamentalPoint_two` (`= n - r 0 - r 1`) etc.
   As a by-product, `r 0 + r 1 ≤ n` etc. under `hX`.
4. `IsGoodPosition.properTransform`: `IsGoodPosition F → IsGoodPosition F'`.
   No other hypothesis is needed, since good position gives `hX`.

(1) holds for every nonzero form, and (2)–(4) for every form with no
exceptional component, irreducible or not. This is the generality the
atlas's formalization notes ask for (Problem 7.16 (a)).

### The milestone (`Birational.lean`)

- `properTransform_spec` (`[IsAlgClosed k]`, `F.IsHomogeneous n`,
  `Irreducible F`, `hX : ∀ i, ¬ X i ∣ F`). It is one theorem, the conjunction
  of
  1. `quadraticTransform_eq`, `∀ i, ¬ X i ∣ F'` and
     `∀ i, multiplicity (X i) (F^Q) = (mult F (fundamentalPoint i)).toNat`;
  2. `F'.IsHomogeneous (2 * n - r 0 - r 1 - r 2)`, `Irreducible F'`,
     `properTransform F' = F`, the closure equality, `bijOn_quadraticMap`,
     the `MapsTo` of `C' ∖ {P, P', P''}` into `C`, and
     `Nonempty (functionField F ≃ₐ[k] functionField F')` (inside a
     `haveI` for the `Fact`), or the explicit equivalence with its `_mk`
     lemma;
  3. the three multiplicities;
  4. `IsGoodPosition F → IsGoodPosition F'`.

  Its docstring ends with "Milestone: *Proper transform of a plane curve under
  the standard quadratic transformation*." This is the atlas's statement. Its
  hypothesis "`F` not an exceptional line" is `hX` for irreducible `F`. Its
  `[IsAlgClosed k]` is used only by the closure. The general forms above
  carry their own, weaker hypotheses.

## Notes

- **No errata.** The atlas states (1)–(3) for irreducible `F` that is not an
  exceptional line, and its formalization notes extend them to forms with no
  exceptional component. T2 proves that generality or more: (1) for every
  nonzero form. The hypothesis `hX` is needed for `(F')' = F` and (3), as
  the example `F = X` shows ([the route](#the-mathematics-and-the-route)).
  T2 adds no entry to `ERRATA.md`.
- **Why `toNat`.** `mult` is `ℕ∞`-valued, and is `⊤` only at `F = 0`.
  Exponents and degrees are natural numbers, so the definition uses
  `(mult F P).toNat`. The statements of (3) stay in `ℕ∞`, as an addition
  rather than a subtraction, so that no truncation hides a wrong value.
- **For T3.** T3 needs, from T2: `fundamentalPoint`, `fundamentalTriangle`,
  `quadraticTransform`, `properTransform`, `quadraticTransform_eq`,
  `IsHomogeneous.properTransform`, `irreducible_properTransform`,
  `properTransform_properTransform`, the multiplicities at the fundamental
  points, `IsGoodPosition` and `IsGoodPosition.properTransform`,
  `quadraticMap` with `quadraticMap_mem_zeroLocus_properTransform_iff` and
  `bijOn_quadraticMap`, the expansion `quadraticTransform_eq_sum` and the
  restriction `aeval_X_two_eq_zero_properTransform`. Every one is listed
  above, with the names `design/T3.md` uses where it gives one.
  `design/T3.md`'s mentions of "unless T2 has them" are resolved: T2 has
  them, in the files given here.
