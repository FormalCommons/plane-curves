## Conventions

These answer the questions of #169: what a linear subvariety, a line and a
hyperplane are in Lean, where the zero-locus statements go, and in which form
changes of coordinates appear.

- **Linear subvarieties are submodules.** A linear subvariety of `ℙ K V` is
  `(p.projectivization : Set (ℙ K V))` for `p : Submodule K V`, Mathlib's
  `Submodule.projectivization`, the inverse of the order isomorphism
  `Projectivization.Subspace.submodule`
  (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/Subspace.lean`). Its
  dimension `m` is given by `Module.finrank K p = m + 1`; Fulton's
  `m = -1` is `p = ⊥`, the empty set. There is no predicate "linear
  subvariety of dimension `m`" and no dimension function: statements say
  `finrank K p = m + 1`. Statements are about `Submodule`, not
  `Projectivization.Subspace`, because Mathlib's own uniqueness of lines
  (`Projectivization.line_unique`), Tau Ceti's
  `Projectivization.submodule_map` and the dimension theory are about
  submodules; the order isomorphism converts when a `Subspace` is wanted.
- **Lines** are submodules with `finrank K p = 2`. The line through two
  points `x ≠ y` is `x.submodule ⊔ y.submodule`, whose projectivization is
  `Projectivization.Subspace.span {x, y}` under the order isomorphism.
- **Hyperplanes** are `(LinearMap.ker f).projectivization` for
  `f : Module.Dual K V`, `f ≠ 0`. A point `x` is on it iff `f x.rep = 0`.
- **Linear forms** in the polynomial layer are written
  `∑ i, C (a i) * X i` with a coefficient vector `a : Fin (n + 1) → k`, as
  Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.polynomialAction_sum_C_mul_X`) and `DESIGN.md`'s
  `tangentLine` do; there is no definition for them. Their functional is
  Mathlib's `dotProductEquiv k (Fin (n + 1)) a`, `v ↦ a ⬝ᵥ v`
  (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Dual.lean`). A form `H` with
  `H.IsHomogeneous 1` is rewritten into this shape by piece 3's
  `IsHomogeneous.eq_sum_C_mul_X`.
- **Changes of coordinates.** In the abstract statements a change of
  coordinates is `e : V ≃ₗ[K] V`, acting on points by
  `Projectivization.map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) e.injective` and on subspaces by
  `Submodule.map`. Mathlib's group action of
  `LinearMap.GeneralLinearGroup K V` is the same map
  (`Projectivization.generalLinearGroup_smul_def`). In coordinates, as
  `DESIGN.md` fixes, it is a matrix `M` with
  `hM : Function.Injective M.mulVecLin`, acting on points by
  `Projectivization.map M.mulVecLin hM` and on polynomials by Tau Ceti's
  `MvPolynomial.linearSubst M`; P4's `zeroLocus_linearSubst` is the
  bridge (`V(linearSubst M '' S) = map M.mulVecLin hM ⁻¹' V(S)`, Fulton's
  `V^T`). Existence statements in coordinates therefore read
  `∃ (M : Matrix (Fin (n + 1)) (Fin (n + 1)) k) (hM : Function.Injective M.mulVecLin), …`.
  Invertibility, when needed, is Mathlib's `Matrix.mulVec_injective_iff_isUnit`.
  A matrix is obtained from `e` as `LinearMap.toMatrix' e`, with
  `Matrix.toLin'_toMatrix'`; this is a step inside proofs, not a lemma.
- **Where the zero loci go** (departure 5 of `DESIGN.md`): everything that
  mentions only `Projectivization`, `Submodule` and `Module.Dual` is in
  `LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/LinearSubspace.lean`, in Mathlib's
  namespaces. The translation into zero loci of linear forms, and every
  statement with `linearSubst` or `MvPolynomial`, is in a new file
  `AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/LinearSubvariety.lean`, in
  `PlaneCurves.ProjectiveSpace`. Only that file needs P4.

## Generality

The atlas says that (a)–(h) hold over any field, that "`V` is a variety"
needs `k` infinite, and that (i) needs `k` infinite. The statements take
the weakest hypotheses the proofs use:

| Statements | Hypotheses |
|---|---|
| images, lines, (c), (d), (e), the pair of lines, normal form of subspaces | `[DivisionRing K]` |
| anything with `Module.Dual`: hyperplanes, (b), "not on a hyperplane" | `[Field K]`, so that `Module.Dual K V` is a `K`-module with Mathlib's annihilator theory; a lemma whose Mathlib ingredients hold over a division ring (such as `Module.Dual.finrank_ker_add_one_of_ne_zero`) takes `[DivisionRing K]` |
| (i) | `[Field K] [Infinite K]` |
| zero loci, `linearSubst`, (h) | `[Field k]` |
| `V` is a variety | `[Field k] [Infinite k]` |

`[FiniteDimensional K V]` appears only where a dimension count needs it.
No statement needs `[IsAlgClosed k]`. Fulton's (c)–(e) and (h) speak of
`ℙ²` or of lines; statements are for `ℙ K V` with `finrank K V = 3` where
the plane matters ((e)) and for any `V` otherwise.

## Definitions

None. Everything P8 needs is Mathlib's: `Projectivization`,
`Submodule.projectivization`, `Projectivization.submodule`,
`Projectivization.Independent`, `Module.Dual`, `dotProductEquiv`,
`Projectivization.map`, and Tau Ceti's `MvPolynomial.linearSubst`. In
particular there is no `Projectivization.Subspace.map`; images of
subspaces are `Submodule.map`, and a lemma (piece 1) says that the image of
a projectivization is the projectivization of the image.

### Piece 1: linear subvarieties, dimension, lines (about 250 lines)

In `LinearAlgebra/FiniteDimensional/Lemmas.lean`, `[DivisionRing K]`
except where marked:

- `LinearIndependent.exists_linearEquiv_apply_eq {ι} [Finite ι] {v w : ι → V}
  (hv : LinearIndependent K v) (hw : LinearIndependent K w) :
  ∃ e : V ≃ₗ[K] V, ∀ i, e (v i) = w i`.
  Proof: `hv.linearCombinationEquiv.symm ≪≫ₗ hw.linearCombinationEquiv`
  between the spans, extended by Mathlib's
  `Submodule.exists_linearEquiv_restrict_eq`, as Mathlib's proof of
  `Projectivization.linearEquiv_is_two_pretransitive` does for two vectors.
  No finite dimension of `V` is needed.
- `Submodule.exists_linearEquiv_map_eq {p q : Submodule K V} [FiniteDimensional K p]
  [FiniteDimensional K q] (h : finrank K p = finrank K q) :
  ∃ e : V ≃ₗ[K] V, p.map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) = q`.
  Proof: `LinearEquiv.ofFinrankEq` and `Submodule.exists_linearEquiv_restrict_eq`.
  The converse is Mathlib's `LinearEquiv.finrank_map_eq`.
- `[Field K] [FiniteDimensional K V]`:
  `Submodule.finrank_add_one_eq_iff_exists_ker_eq (p : Submodule K V) :
  finrank K p + 1 = finrank K V ↔ ∃ f : Module.Dual K V, f ≠ 0 ∧ LinearMap.ker f = p`.
  `←` is Mathlib's `Module.Dual.finrank_ker_add_one_of_ne_zero`; `→` takes
  `f` with `p ≤ ker f` from `Submodule.exists_le_ker_of_lt_top` and
  compares dimensions. With `finrank K V = 3` this is "in `ℙ²` lines are
  hyperplanes" (part of (c)).

In `LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/LinearSubspace.lean`, `[DivisionRing K]`:

- `Submodule.image_map_projectivization (f : V →ₗ[K] W) (hf : Injective f) (p : Submodule K V) :
  map f hf '' p.projectivization = (p.map f).projectivization` (as sets),
  and `Submodule.preimage_map_projectivization (f) (hf) (q : Submodule K W) :
  map f hf ⁻¹' q.projectivization = (q.comap f).projectivization`.
  First half of (a): a change of coordinates carries a linear subvariety to
  a linear subvariety, of the same dimension by `LinearEquiv.finrank_map_eq`.
- `Submodule.projectivization_nonempty_iff (p : Submodule K V) :
  (p.projectivization : Set (ℙ K V)).Nonempty ↔ p ≠ ⊥`, from
  Mathlib's `Projectivization.Subspace.bot_coe` (Fulton's `m = -1` iff
  `V = ∅`).
- `Submodule.exists_linearEquiv_image_map_projectivization_eq_iff [FiniteDimensional K V]
  (p q : Submodule K V) : (∃ e : V ≃ₗ[K] V, map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) e.injective '' p.projectivization =
  q.projectivization) ↔ finrank K p = finrank K q`.
  This is (a) without equations: some change of coordinates carries
  `V` onto any other linear subvariety of the same dimension, in
  particular onto the coordinate one, and the dimension does not depend on
  the change. Proof: the previous lemmas, `Submodule.exists_linearEquiv_map_eq`,
  and injectivity of `Submodule.projectivization` (an order isomorphism)
  with `SetLike.coe_injective`.
- (c) `Projectivization.mem_projectivization_sup_iff {a b : V} (ha : a ≠ 0) (hb : b ≠ 0) (x : ℙ K V) :
  x ∈ ((mk K a ha).submodule ⊔ (mk K b hb).submodule).projectivization ↔
  ∃ (l m : K) (h : l • a + m • b ≠ 0), x = mk K (l • a + m • b) h`.
  Proof: `Submodule.mem_sup`, `Submodule.mem_span_singleton`,
  `Projectivization.submodule_mk`. When `mk K a ha ≠ mk K b hb`,
  `l • a + m • b ≠ 0` iff `(l, m) ≠ (0, 0)` by `LinearIndependent.pair_iff`
  and `Projectivization.linearIndependent_pair_iff_ne`; the docstring says
  so, and no separate lemma is added unless P9 needs it.
- (c) `Projectivization.finrank_submodule_sup {x y : ℙ K V} (h : x ≠ y) :
  finrank K (x.submodule ⊔ y.submodule) = 2`, from
  `Projectivization.linearIndependent_pair_iff_ne` and
  `finrank_span_eq_card`. With Mathlib's `Projectivization.line_unique`
  this says: a line is a linear subvariety of dimension 1, and each line is
  the line through any two of its points.
- (c) Changes of coordinates carry the line through `x`, `y` to the line
  through the images: no new lemma; it is `Submodule.map_sup` with Tau
  Ceti's `Projectivization.submodule_map`
  (`TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/Basic.lean`). P9's milestone
  states it with these.
- (b) `[Field K] [FiniteDimensional K V]`:
  `Projectivization.exists_forall_dual_apply_rep_eq_zero {ι} [Fintype ι] (f : ι → Module.Dual K V)
  (h : Fintype.card ι < finrank K V) : ∃ x : ℙ K V, ∀ i, f i x.rep = 0`.
  Proof: Mathlib's `LinearMap.ker_ne_bot_of_finrank_lt` for
  `LinearMap.pi f : V →ₗ[K] ι → K`. With `finrank K V = n + 1` and
  `card ι = m ≤ n`: any `m ≤ n` hyperplanes of `ℙⁿ` meet.

### Piece 2: general position (about 250 lines)

In `LinearSubspace.lean`:

- (d) `Projectivization.exists_linearEquiv_map_eq_of_independent {ι} [Finite ι] {p q : ι → ℙ K V}
  (hp : Independent p) (hq : Independent q) :
  ∃ e : V ≃ₗ[K] V, ∀ i, map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) e.injective (p i) = q i`.
  Proof: `Projectivization.independent_iff` gives independent
  representatives; apply `LinearIndependent.exists_linearEquiv_apply_eq`
  and `Projectivization.map_mk`, `mk_rep`. This generalizes Mathlib's
  two-point `Projectivization.linearEquiv_is_two_pretransitive` to any
  finite independent family; it does not restate it.
- (d) `[Field K] [FiniteDimensional K V]`:
  `Projectivization.independent_iff_forall_dual {ι} [Fintype ι] (p : ι → ℙ K V)
  (h : Fintype.card ι = finrank K V) :
  Independent p ↔ ∀ f : Module.Dual K V, (∀ i, f (p i).rep = 0) → f = 0`:
  `n + 1` points are independent iff they are not on a hyperplane. Proof:
  `linearIndependent_iff_card_eq_finrank_span` (span `= ⊤`) and
  `Submodule.dualAnnihilator_eq_bot_iff`.
- (c), the analogue of Problem 2.15(d):
  `Projectivization.exists_linearEquiv_map_eq_of_finrank_eq_two {x x' : ℙ K V}
  {L₁ L₂ L₁' L₂' : Submodule K V} (h₁ : finrank K L₁ = 2) (h₂ : finrank K L₂ = 2)
  (h₁' : finrank K L₁' = 2) (h₂' : finrank K L₂' = 2) (hL : L₁ ≠ L₂) (hL' : L₁' ≠ L₂')
  (hx₁ : x ∈ L₁.projectivization) (hx₂ : x ∈ L₂.projectivization)
  (hx₁' : x' ∈ L₁'.projectivization) (hx₂' : x' ∈ L₂'.projectivization) :
  ∃ e : V ≃ₗ[K] V, map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) e.injective x = x' ∧
    L₁.map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) = L₁' ∧ L₂.map (e : V →ₗ[K] V) = L₂'`.
  Proof: a line has a point `yᵢ ≠ x` (it has two independent vectors);
  `![x, y₁, y₂]` is independent, since otherwise `y₂` is on the line
  `x.submodule ⊔ y₁.submodule = L₁` (`line_unique`) and then `L₂ = L₁`;
  likewise for the primed points; apply the frame lemma and
  `Submodule.map_sup` with `Projectivization.submodule_map` and
  `line_unique`. Stated in `ℙ K V` of any dimension; Fulton's case is
  `finrank K V = 3`.
- (e) `[FiniteDimensional K V]`:
  `Projectivization.existsUnique_mem_of_finrank_eq_two (hV : finrank K V = 3)
  {p q : Submodule K V} (hp : finrank K p = 2) (hq : finrank K q = 2) (hpq : p ≠ q) :
  ∃! x : ℙ K V, x ∈ p.projectivization ∧ x ∈ q.projectivization`.
  Existence: `Submodule.finrank_sup_add_finrank_inf_eq` gives
  `finrank K (p ⊓ q) ≥ 1`; uniqueness: two distinct common points would give
  `p = q` by `line_unique`. Mathlib's `Projectivization.cross` and
  `cross_orthogonal_left/right` give the point in coordinates; piece 4 uses
  them for the equation form.
- (i) `[Field K] [Infinite K]`:
  `Projectivization.exists_dual_mem_forall_apply_rep_ne_zero (W : Submodule K (Module.Dual K V))
  {S : Set (ℙ K V)} (hS : S.Finite) (h : ∀ x ∈ S, ∃ f ∈ W, f x.rep ≠ 0) :
  ∃ f ∈ W, ∀ x ∈ S, f x.rep ≠ 0`.
  Proof: Mathlib's `Subspace.biUnion_ne_univ_of_top_notMem`
  (`Mathlib/GroupTheory/CosetCover.lean`) in the module `W`, for the
  finitely many subspaces `{f ∈ W | f x.rep = 0}`, each proper by `h`.
  Two corollaries, both used later:
  - `Projectivization.exists_dual_forall_apply_rep_ne_zero [Nontrivial V] {S} (hS : S.Finite) :
    ∃ f : Module.Dual K V, f ≠ 0 ∧ ∀ x ∈ S, f x.rep ≠ 0`
    (`W = ⊤`, with Mathlib's `Module.forall_dual_apply_eq_zero_iff`; add
    one more subspace, `⊥`, to get `f ≠ 0` when `S = ∅`): some hyperplane
    passes through none of finitely many points. This is (i).
  - `Projectivization.exists_dual_apply_rep_eq_zero_forall_ne_zero (x : ℙ K V) {S} (hS : S.Finite)
    (hx : x ∉ S) : ∃ f : Module.Dual K V, f x.rep = 0 ∧ ∀ y ∈ S, f y.rep ≠ 0`
    (`W = (K ∙ x.rep).dualAnnihilator`, with `Submodule.exists_le_ker_of_notMem`
    for `h`): a hyperplane through `x` missing finitely many other points.
    In `ℙ²` this is the line through `P_i` avoiding the other points that
    Q10's proof of Theorem 1 of §5.2 uses.

### Piece 3: zero loci of linear forms (about 300 lines)

In `RingTheory/MvPolynomial/LinearForm.lean`, `{σ R} [Fintype σ] [CommSemiring R]`:

- `eval_sum_C_mul_X (a x : σ → R) : eval x (∑ i, C (a i) * X i) = a ⬝ᵥ x`.
- `isHomogeneous_sum_C_mul_X (a : σ → R) : (∑ i, C (a i) * X i).IsHomogeneous 1`.
- `coeff_single_sum_C_mul_X (a : σ → R) (i : σ) :
  (∑ j, C (a j) * X j).coeff (Finsupp.single i 1) = a i`, hence
  `sum_C_mul_X_injective : Function.Injective fun a : σ → R ↦ ∑ i, C (a i) * X i`.
- `IsHomogeneous.eq_sum_C_mul_X {φ : MvPolynomial σ R} (h : φ.IsHomogeneous 1) :
  φ = ∑ i, C (φ.coeff (Finsupp.single i 1)) * X i`
  (a monomial of degree 1 is `single i 1`).
- `linearSubst_sum_C_mul_X (M : Matrix σ σ R) (a : σ → R) :
  linearSubst M (∑ i, C (a i) * X i) = ∑ j, C ((a ᵥ* M) j) * X j`,
  from Tau Ceti's `polynomialAction_sum_C_mul_X` and
  `polynomialAction_eq_linearSubst_transpose`, or directly from
  `linearSubst_X`.

In `RingTheory/MvPolynomial/VanishingIdeal.lean` (A1's file):

- `MvPolynomial.isPrime_vanishingIdeal_submodule {σ k} [Finite σ] [Field k] [Infinite k]
  (p : Submodule k (σ → k)) : (vanishingIdeal k (p : Set (σ → k))).IsPrime`.
  `[Finite σ]` makes `p` finite-dimensional; the only use is `σ = Fin (n + 1)`.
  Proof: for a basis `b : Fin r → p` (`r = finrank k p`), the algebra map
  `φ := aeval fun i ↦ ∑ j, C ((b j : σ → k) i) * X j : MvPolynomial σ k →ₐ[k] MvPolynomial (Fin r) k`
  satisfies `eval y (φ f) = eval (∑ j, y j • b j) f`, so `f` vanishes on
  `p` iff `φ f` vanishes on `k^r`, iff `φ f = 0` (Mathlib's
  `MvPolynomial.funext`, infinite `k`). Hence the ideal is `RingHom.ker φ`,
  prime since `MvPolynomial (Fin r) k` is a domain.

In `AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/LinearSubvariety.lean`,
`{k} [Field k] {n : ℕ}`; write `L a` for `∑ i, C (a i) * X i` in prose only:

- `zeroLocus_image_sum_C_mul_X (s : Set (Fin (n + 1) → k)) :
  zeroLocus ((fun a ↦ ∑ i, C (a i) * X i) '' s) =
  ((Submodule.span k s).map (dotProductEquiv k (Fin (n + 1)) : _ →ₗ[k] _)).dualCoannihilator.projectivization`.
  Proof: P4's `mk_mem_zeroLocus_iff` (linear forms are homogeneous),
  `eval_sum_C_mul_X`, `Submodule.mem_dualCoannihilator`, and
  `Submodule.span_induction`. Corollary for one form,
  `zeroLocus_singleton_sum_C_mul_X (a) :
  zeroLocus {∑ i, C (a i) * X i} = (LinearMap.ker (dotProductEquiv k (Fin (n + 1)) a)).projectivization`:
  for `a ≠ 0`, `V(L a)` is a hyperplane.
- (a) dimension: `finrank_add_finrank_span_of_zeroLocus_eq {s} {p : Submodule k (Fin (n + 1) → k)}
  (h : zeroLocus ((fun a ↦ ∑ i, C (a i) * X i) '' s) = p.projectivization) :
  finrank k p + finrank k (Submodule.span k s) = n + 1`
  (`Subspace.finrank_add_finrank_dualCoannihilator_eq`,
  `LinearEquiv.finrank_map_eq`, injectivity of `Submodule.projectivization`).
  So `V(H₁, …, H_r)` has dimension `n − rank (H₁, …, H_r)`.
- (a) converse: `exists_finset_zeroLocus_image_sum_C_mul_X_eq (p : Submodule k (Fin (n + 1) → k)) :
  ∃ s : Finset (Fin (n + 1) → k), s.card + finrank k p = n + 1 ∧
    zeroLocus ((fun a ↦ ∑ i, C (a i) * X i) '' ↑s) = p.projectivization`:
  the linear subvarieties are exactly the projectivized subspaces. Proof: a
  basis of `p.dualAnnihilator`, pulled back by `dotProductEquiv`, and
  Mathlib's `Subspace.dualAnnihilator_dualCoannihilator_eq`.
- (b) in equations: `zeroLocus_image_sum_C_mul_X_nonempty (s : Finset (Fin (n + 1) → k))
  (h : s.card ≤ n) : (zeroLocus ((fun a ↦ ∑ i, C (a i) * X i) '' ↑s)).Nonempty`,
  from the dimension count (`finrank_span_finset_le_card` and
  `Subspace.finrank_add_finrank_dualCoannihilator_eq`), so it does not wait for piece 1.
- `cone_projectivization (p : Submodule k (Fin (n + 1) → k)) :
  cone (p.projectivization : Set _) = p` (P4's `cone`).
- (a) variety: `isPrime_vanishingIdeal_projectivization [Infinite k] {p : Submodule k (Fin (n + 1) → k)}
  (hp : p ≠ ⊥) : (vanishingIdeal (p.projectivization : Set _)).toIdeal.IsPrime`,
  from P4's `vanishingIdeal_cone`, `cone_projectivization` and
  `MvPolynomial.isPrime_vanishingIdeal_submodule`. With
  `zeroLocus_vanishingIdeal_projectivization : zeroLocus (vanishingIdeal (p.projectivization : Set _)) = p.projectivization`
  (over any field: the linear forms of the converse above lie in the ideal), a
  nonempty linear subvariety is a projective variety in the sense of
  P6/P14 (algebraic with prime ideal).

### Piece 4: coordinates and the plane (about 250 lines)

In `LinearSubvariety.lean`:

## Notes

- **No error in the atlas.** Every statement of (a)–(e), (h), (i) holds as
  the atlas states it, in the generality given above. `ERRATA.md` gets no
  entry. The reading "two distinct lines through `P` and two through `P'`"
  of the analogue of Problem 2.15(d) is the atlas's own; P8 proves it in any
  dimension.
- **Why submodules, not a new type.** A structure "linear subvariety" would
  duplicate `Projectivization.Subspace`, which Mathlib already has with its
  lattice. A predicate on sets ("is the zero locus of linear forms") is
  equivalent to being a projectivized subspace by piece 3, and every proof
  goes through the subspace anyway, as the atlas advises ("Work with
  subspaces, not equations").
- **Why a second file for zero loci.** Departure 5 of `DESIGN.md` keeps
  `LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/` free of `MvPolynomial`, so that pieces 1
  and 2 can move to Mathlib as they are, and they do not wait for P4.
