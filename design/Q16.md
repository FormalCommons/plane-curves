# Part Q16: the Hessian of a form

This is the detailed design of part **Q16** of [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md):
the determinant of `TauCeti.hessian MvPolynomial.pderiv F`, its
homogeneity, its covariance under linear changes of coordinates, and the
identity that relates it to `f`, `g` in the affine chart `Z = 1`. It
supplies steps (a)–(c) of the atlas's outline for the milestone **5-hessian**
([`atlas/02-projective-plane-curves.md`](https://github.com/FormalCommons/plane-curves-atlas/blob/main/atlas/02-projective-plane-curves.md),
milestone "Hessian criterion for flexes and the nine flexes of a nonsingular
cubic"; [Fulton2008], §5.3, Problem 5.23). Part Q17 proves the milestone
from them. Q16 proves no milestone itself.

Everything here is polynomial algebra over a commutative ring. Nothing uses
a field, points of `ℙ²`, or a characteristic hypothesis. The
characteristic enters only in Q17, where `(n - 1)²`, `2` and `6` are
inverted (see the atlas's formalization notes).

## Notation

Throughout, `R` is a commutative ring (`[CommRing R]`, which
`TauCeti.hessian` needs), `σ` a `Fintype` with `DecidableEq`, and
`F : MvPolynomial σ R`. Write

```lean
H F := (TauCeti.hessian MvPolynomial.pderiv F).det
```

only in prose: as `DESIGN.md` says, the Hessian gets **no new
definition**, and every statement writes
`(TauCeti.hessian MvPolynomial.pderiv F).det` or the matrix
`TauCeti.hessian MvPolynomial.pderiv F` itself, whose entries are
`pderiv i (pderiv j F)` by `TauCeti.hessian_apply` (`@[simp]`). Write
`Fᵢ = pderiv i F` and `Fᵢⱼ = pderiv i (pderiv j F)`. For `σ = Fin 3`,
`X 0, X 1, X 2` are `X, Y, Z`.

## What exists and is used

Located in the pinned sources (Tau Ceti `cd41bff`, Mathlib `048fffa`):

- `TauCeti.hessian`, `TauCeti.hessian_apply`
  (`TauCeti/RingTheory/LocalRing/Hessian.lean`).
- `MvPolynomial.pderiv`, `MvPolynomial.pderiv_comm`, `pderiv_X`,
  `pderiv_mul`, `pderiv_C_mul` (`Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/PDeriv.lean`).
- `MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.pderiv` (degree `n - 1`) and Euler's identity
  `MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.sum_X_mul_pderiv`
  (`∑ i, X i * pderiv i φ = n • φ`), in
  `Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/EulerIdentity.lean`.
- `MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.prod`, `.sum`, `.mul`
  (`Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Homogeneous.lean`), for the homogeneity
  of a determinant through `Matrix.det_apply`.
- `Matrix.det_updateCol_sum`, `Matrix.det_updateRow_sum` (replacing a column
  or row by a linear combination of all of them multiplies the determinant
  by the coefficient of the replaced one), `Matrix.det_updateCol_smul`,
  `Matrix.det_updateRow_smul`, `Matrix.det_mul`, `Matrix.det_transpose`,
  `Matrix.det_fin_three`, `AlgHom.map_det`, `RingHom.map_det`
  (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Determinant/Basic.lean`).
- `MvPolynomial.linearSubst` with `pderiv_linearSubst` (the chain rule
  `pderiv j (linearSubst M p) = ∑ i, C (M i j) * linearSubst M (pderiv i p)`),
  `eval_linearSubst`, `IsHomogeneous.linearSubst`
  (`TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/LinearSubst.lean`).
- `TauCeti.dehomogenize` with `pderiv_dehomogenize`
  (`pderiv j (dehomogenize i p) = dehomogenize i (pderiv (i.succAbove j) p)`),
  `dehomogenize_X` (`TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Dehomogenization.lean`).

Searched and not found (so they are added here): homogeneity of a
determinant of homogeneous entries (Mathlib has it only for the universal
characteristic polynomial, `Matrix.charpoly.univ`), any lemma on
`TauCeti.hessian` of a polynomial (Tau Ceti's Hessians are used only in
local rings), the Hessian under `linearSubst`, and the chart identity.

From the parts it needs: nothing. `DESIGN.md` lists Q2 as a need, but no
statement here mentions simple points or `IsNonsingular`, so the part can be
built before Q2. Q17, which uses both, needs Q2 through Q13 anyway.

## Piece 1: `RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Hessian.lean`

About 200–300 lines. Namespace `MvPolynomial`, variables
`{R σ : Type*} [CommRing R] [Fintype σ] [DecidableEq σ]` (drop the
`Fintype` and `DecidableEq` where a statement does not need them).

**Symmetry.**

- `isSymm_hessian_pderiv (F) : (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).IsSymm`, from
  `pderiv_comm`. (No `Fintype` needed.)

**Determinants of homogeneous matrices.**

- `isHomogeneous_det {ι} [Fintype ι] [DecidableEq ι] {M : Matrix ι ι (MvPolynomial σ R)} {d : ℕ}
  (h : ∀ i j, (M i j).IsHomogeneous d) : M.det.IsHomogeneous (Fintype.card ι * d)`,
  by `Matrix.det_apply`, `IsHomogeneous.sum`, `IsHomogeneous.prod` and
  `Finset.sum_const`. This is a general lemma about determinants, but it
  lives here until another part needs it.

**Homogeneity.** For `hF : F.IsHomogeneous n`:

- `IsHomogeneous.hessian_apply (hF) (i j) : (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F i j).IsHomogeneous (n - 2)`
  (two applications of `IsHomogeneous.pderiv`, and `n - 1 - 1 = n - 2`).
- `IsHomogeneous.det_hessian (hF) : (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).det.IsHomogeneous (Fintype.card σ * (n - 2))`.
  For `σ = Fin 3` this is the atlas's degree `3(n - 2)`. The atlas's "or 0"
  needs no statement, because `0` is homogeneous of every degree.

**Euler's identity for the Hessian** (step (b) of the outline). For
`hF : F.IsHomogeneous n`:

- `IsHomogeneous.hessian_mulVec_X (hF) : TauCeti.hessian pderiv F *ᵥ X = (n - 1) • fun i ↦ pderiv i F`,
  i.e. `∑ j, Fᵢⱼ * X j = (n - 1) • Fᵢ`. Proof: Euler's identity for the form
  `pderiv i F` of degree `n - 1`, and `pderiv_comm`. Add the `vecMul`
  form `X ᵥ* TauCeti.hessian pderiv F = …` from the symmetry.
- `IsHomogeneous.X_mul_det_hessian (hF) (j) : X j * (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).det = (n - 1) • ((TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).updateCol j fun i ↦ pderiv i F).det`.
  Proof: `Matrix.det_updateCol_sum` with coefficients `X`, then
  `hessian_mulVec_X` and `Matrix.det_updateCol_smul`.
- `IsHomogeneous.sq_X_mul_det_hessian (hF) (j) : X j ^ 2 * (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).det = (n - 1) • (((TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).updateCol j fun i ↦ pderiv i F).updateRow j fun k ↦ if k = j then n • F else (n - 1) • pderiv k F).det`.
  Proof: the previous lemma, then `Matrix.det_updateRow_sum` with
  coefficients `X` on the matrix with the column replaced. Its row `j`
  becomes `∑ i, X i • (row i)`, which is `(n - 1) • Fₖ` off the diagonal by
  `hessian_mulVec_X` and `n • F` at `j` by Euler's identity.

  This bordered-determinant form is what the plane case expands (piece 2).
  It is stated for any `σ` because the proof is no harder there.

**Covariance** (step (a) of the outline). For `M : Matrix σ σ R`:

- `hessian_linearSubst (M) (F) : TauCeti.hessian pderiv (linearSubst M F) = (M.map C)ᵀ * (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).map (linearSubst M) * M.map C`.
  Proof: `ext a b`, `pderiv_linearSubst` twice, `Matrix.mul_apply`,
  `Finset.mul_sum`. The entry is `∑ i, ∑ l, M i a * M l b * linearSubst M Fᵢₗ`.
- `det_hessian_linearSubst (M) (F) : (TauCeti.hessian pderiv (linearSubst M F)).det = C (M.det ^ 2) * linearSubst M (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).det`.
  This is the atlas's "the Hessian of `F^T` is `(det T)² H^T`". Proof:
  `Matrix.det_mul`, `Matrix.det_transpose`, `RingHom.map_det` for `C`, and
  `AlgHom.map_det` for `linearSubst M`.
- `eval_det_hessian_linearSubst (M) (F) (v) : eval v (TauCeti.hessian pderiv (linearSubst M F)).det = M.det ^ 2 * eval (M *ᵥ v) (TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).det`,
  from the previous lemma and `eval_linearSubst`. This is the form in which
  Q17 moves a point to `[0:0:1]`: for invertible `M`, `v` is a zero of the
  Hessian of `linearSubst M F` iff `M *ᵥ v` is a zero of the Hessian of `F`.

**Charts.** For `F : MvPolynomial (Fin (m + 1)) R` and `i : Fin (m + 1)`:

- `hessian_dehomogenize (i) (F) : TauCeti.hessian pderiv (TauCeti.dehomogenize i F) = ((TauCeti.hessian pderiv F).submatrix i.succAbove i.succAbove).map (TauCeti.dehomogenize i)`,
  by `pderiv_dehomogenize` twice. In the chart `Z = 1`, `f_xx`, `f_xy`,
  `f_yy` are the dehomogenized `F_XX`, `F_XY`, `F_YY`.

## Piece 2: `PlaneCurve/Projective/Hessian/Basic.lean`

About 150–250 lines. Imports piece 1. Variables `{R : Type*} [CommRing R]`,
`{F : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R} {n : ℕ} (hF : F.IsHomogeneous n)`. The
statements are about `MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R`, so they are in the namespace
`MvPolynomial` (dot notation on `hF`), as `AGENTS.md` asks for declarations
about Mathlib types. The file docstring explains that these are the
plane-curve identities of Problem 5.23 and cites [Fulton2008].

- **The plane identity** `IsHomogeneous.sq_X_two_mul_det_hessian (hF)`:

  ```
  X 2 ^ 2 * (hessian pderiv F).det
    = ((n - 1 : ℕ) : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R) *
        ((n : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R) * F * (F₀₀ * F₁₁ - F₀₁ ^ 2)
          - ((n - 1 : ℕ) : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R) *
              (F₁ ^ 2 * F₀₀ + F₀ ^ 2 * F₁₁ - 2 * F₀ * F₁ * F₀₁))
  ```

  with `Fᵢ = pderiv i F` and `Fᵢⱼ = pderiv i (pderiv j F)` written out.
  Proof: `sq_X_mul_det_hessian hF 2`, `Matrix.det_fin_three`, the `simp`
  lemmas of `updateRow`/`updateCol`, `pderiv_comm` for `F₁₀ = F₀₁`, then
  `nsmul_eq_mul` and `ring`. By hand, the bordered matrix is
  `!![F₀₀, F₀₁, F₀; F₀₁, F₁₁, F₁; (n-1)F₀, (n-1)F₁, nF]`. Its determinant
  is `nF(F₀₀F₁₁ − F₀₁²) − (n−1)(F₀₀F₁² − 2F₀₁F₀F₁ + F₁₁F₀²)`.

- **The chart identity** `IsHomogeneous.dehomogenize_det_hessian (hF)`
  (step (c) of the outline). Let `f = TauCeti.dehomogenize 2 F :
  MvPolynomial (Fin 2) R`, `fᵢ = pderiv i f`, `fᵢⱼ = pderiv i (pderiv j f)`.
  Then

  ```
  TauCeti.dehomogenize 2 (hessian pderiv F).det
    = ((n * (n - 1) : ℕ) : MvPolynomial (Fin 2) R) * f * (f₀₀ * f₁₁ - f₀₁ ^ 2)
      - ((n - 1 : ℕ) : MvPolynomial (Fin 2) R) ^ 2 *
          (f₁ ^ 2 * f₀₀ + f₀ ^ 2 * f₁₁ - 2 * f₀ * f₁ * f₀₁)
  ```

  This is the atlas's `h = n(n−1) f (f_xx f_yy − f_xy²) − (n−1)² g`, with
  `g = f_y² f_xx + f_x² f_yy − 2 f_x f_y f_xy`. It holds over every
  commutative ring and for every `n` (for `n ≤ 1` both sides vanish).
  Proof: apply `TauCeti.dehomogenize 2` to the plane identity. Then
  `dehomogenize_X` sends `X 2` to `1`, and `pderiv_dehomogenize` turns the
  dehomogenized `Fᵢ`, `Fᵢⱼ` (`i, j ∈ {0, 1}`, with `(2 : Fin 3).succAbove`
  the identity on `0, 1`) into `fᵢ`, `fᵢⱼ`.

- **The form Q17 uses** `IsHomogeneous.dehomogenize_det_hessian_add_mem_span (hF)`:
  `TauCeti.dehomogenize 2 (hessian pderiv F).det + ((n - 1 : ℕ) : _) ^ 2 * g ∈ Ideal.span {f}`,
  with `g` written out as above. So `h ≡ −(n−1)² g` modulo `f`, and
  Q17 gets `I(P, f ∩ h) = I(P, f ∩ g)` once `n − 1` is a unit, from the
  affine `intersectionNumber_add_mul` and invariance under units.

`g` gets no definition of its own here. It occurs in two statements, and Q17
expands it at a point (step (e)). If Q17 finds that it uses `g` often, it
adds `g` as a definition in its own file, with a `_def` lemma.
