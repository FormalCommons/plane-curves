# Part N7: chord–tangent addition on a Weierstrass cubic

This is the detailed design of part **N7** of [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md)
(stage 3, milestone **5-weierstrass**, *large*; needs N6, N5, L10). It
proves the atlas milestone *Chord–tangent addition on a Weierstrass cubic
agrees with Mathlib's group law* (`atlas/02-projective-plane-curves.md`,
§5.6, Problems 5.38 and 5.40 of [Fulton2008]), and builds the bridge that
`DESIGN.md` asks for: Fulton's group of the simple points of the cubic
`W.polynomial`, with base point `O = [0:1:0]`, is identified with Mathlib's
group `W.Point` by an `AddEquiv`, not by a second group law.

All Mathlib and Tau Ceti declarations named here were located in the pinned
sources (Mathlib `048fffa`, Tau Ceti `cd41bff`); nothing was compiled.
Names of new declarations are proposals, as in `DESIGN.md`.

## Definitions and results

N7 adds two definitions, the bridge maps of pieces 1 and 3, and the
subgroup of piece 4. Everything else is theorems.

### Piece 1: the Weierstrass cubic as a plane curve (any field)

Namespace `WeierstrassCurve.Projective`; `k` any field unless stated.

- `isSimplePoint_polynomial_mk_iff (hv : v ≠ 0) :
  Projective.IsSimplePoint W.polynomial (mk k v hv) ↔ W.Nonsingular v`.
  Proof: Q2's criterion against Mathlib's `nonsingular_iff`, by `Fin 3`
  cases.
- `Point.isSimplePoint_toProjectivization (P : W.Point) :
  IsSimplePoint W.polynomial P.toProjectivization`, and
  `Point.mem_range_toProjectivization_iff_isSimplePoint (P : ℙ k (Fin 3 → k)) :
  P ∈ Set.range Point.toProjectivization ↔ IsSimplePoint W.polynomial P`
  (from Tau Ceti's `mem_range_toProjectivization_iff`). This is "the simple
  points of `F` are exactly the images of Mathlib's nonsingular points".
- **`Point.toSimplePoint : W.Point ≃ {P : ℙ k (Fin 3 → k) // IsSimplePoint W.polynomial P}`**,
  the bijection given by `toProjectivization`, with
  `@[simp] coe_toSimplePoint_apply` (`= P.toProjectivization`) and
  `toSimplePoint_zero` (the point at infinity goes to `O`).
- `tangentLine_polynomial_zero : tangentLine W.polynomial ![0, 1, 0] = X 2`
  (the tangent at `O` is `Z = 0`), from `eval_polynomialX/Y/Z`.
- `isNonsingular_polynomial_iff [PerfectField k] :
  Projective.IsNonsingular W.polynomial ↔ W.Δ ≠ 0`.
  Proof: `←` is Tau Ceti's `equation_iff_nonsingular_of_Δ_ne_zero_of_ne_zero`.
  `→`: if `Δ = 0`, Tau Ceti's `exists_isSingular_of_Δ_eq_zero` gives an
  affine singular point `(x, y)`, and `![x, y, 1]` is a nonzero zero of `F`
  at which every partial derivative vanishes (`nonsingular_some`). The
  atlas states this for algebraically closed `k`; perfect `k` is more
  general. (If N5 has already proved this, piece 1 uses it instead.)
- Irreducibility is `W.prime_polynomial.irreducible` (Tau Ceti), with no
  new lemma.

### Piece 2: lines and the cubic (`[IsAlgClosed k]`)

Namespace `WeierstrassCurve.Projective`. Every nonzero linear form is
`c • Z`, `c • (X − xZ)` or `c • (Y − ℓX − νZ)` with `c ≠ 0` (a private
lemma, by the coefficient of `Y`, then of `X`). Since `F` is irreducible of
degree 3, no line is a component of it, and each line's cycle has degree 3
(N1).

- `intersectionCycle_X_two : intersectionCycle (X 2) W.polynomial = single O 3`.
  Proof: on `Z = 0` the equation forces `X = 0` (`X_eq_zero_of_Z_eq_zero`),
  so the support is `{O}`; the degree is 3.
- `isFlex_zero : IsFlex W.polynomial O` (Q17's predicate), from
  `tangentLine_polynomial_zero` and `intersectionCycle_X_two`.
- `intersectionCycle_X_zero_sub (h : W.toAffine.Equation x y) :
  intersectionCycle (X 0 - C x * X 2) W.polynomial =
  single O 1 + single [x : y : 1] 1 + single [x : W.toAffine.negY x y : 1] 1`
  (vertical lines). Proof: in the chart `Z = 1`, `F(x, Y, 1)` is the monic
  quadratic `(Y − y)(Y − negY x y)` (Mathlib's `negY`), so L10 gives the
  affine part; the line is not the tangent `Z` at the simple point `O`, so
  `I(O, F ∩ L) = 1` (Q7). The two affine points coincide when
  `y = negY x y`, and the formula then has coefficient 2 there.
- `intersectionCycle_X_one_sub (ℓ ν : k) :
  intersectionCycle (X 1 - C ℓ * X 0 - C ν * X 2) W.polynomial =
  ((W.toAffine.polynomial.eval (C ℓ * X + C ν)).roots.map
    fun x ↦ single [x : ℓ * x + ν : 1] 1).sum`
  (non-vertical lines). Proof: the line misses `O`; in the chart `Z = 1` it
  is `t ↦ (t, ℓt + ν)`, and L10 identifies the intersection numbers with the
  root multiplicities of `W(T, ℓT + ν)`, which is the polynomial in Tau
  Ceti's `some_add_some_add_some_eq_zero_iff`. A short lemma identifies
  `dehomogenize 2 W.polynomial` restricted to the line with
  `W.toAffine.polynomial.eval (C ℓ * X + C ν)`.

### Piece 3: collinearity and the bridge (`[IsAlgClosed k]`)

Namespace `WeierstrassCurve.Projective.Point`. No `DecidableEq k` is needed:
Mathlib's `toAffineAddEquiv` is used inside proofs only, under `classical`.
The `Fact` instances `fact_isHomogeneous_polynomial`,
`fact_irreducible_polynomial` and `fact_isSimplePoint_toProjectivization_zero`
(namespace `WeierstrassCurve.Projective`) make `CubicGroup W.polynomial O` a
group.

- `add_add_eq_zero_iff (P Q R : W.Point) :
  P + Q + R = 0 ↔ ∃ L : MvPolynomial (Fin 3) k, L.IsHomogeneous 1 ∧ L ≠ 0 ∧
  intersectionCycle L W.polynomial =
  single P.toProjectivization 1 + single Q.toProjectivization 1 + single R.toProjectivization 1`.
  Proof of `→`: if one of `P, Q, R` is zero, the other two are `S` and `−S`,
  cut out by the line at infinity (`intersectionCycle_X_two`) if `S = 0` and
  by a vertical line (`intersectionCycle_X_zero_sub`, Mathlib's `neg_some`)
  otherwise; if none is, through `toAffineAddEquiv`, Tau Ceti's
  `some_add_some_add_some_eq_zero_iff` gives the non-vertical line and
  `intersectionCycle_X_one_sub` its cycle (this case holds over any field).
  `←` follows from `→` and N6's uniqueness: `R' = φ(P', Q') = (−(P + Q))'`.
- `thirdPoint_toProjectivization (P Q : W.Point) :
  thirdPoint W.polynomial P.toProjectivization Q.toProjectivization =
  (-(P + Q)).toProjectivization` (Fulton's `φ(P, Q) = −(P + Q)`): N6's
  uniqueness statement with `R = −(P + Q)` and the `→` direction above.
- **`Point.toCubicGroup : W.Point ≃+ CubicGroup W.polynomial O`**,
  `toSimplePoint` as an additive equivalence, with
  `@[simp] point_toCubicGroup_apply`. Additivity:
  `P ⊕ Q = φ(O, φ(P, Q)) = φ(0, −(P + Q)) = −(0 + −(P + Q)) = P + Q`, by
  `thirdPoint_toProjectivization` twice and N6's `_def` lemma. No group
  axiom is reproved: `AddEquiv.mk'` needs only the bijection and `map_add`.

### Piece 4: Problems 5.38 and 5.40, and the milestone

- **Problem 5.38** (`WeierstrassCurve.Projective.add_coords_of_a₁_eq_zero_of_a₃_eq_zero`):
  if `W.a₁ = 0`, `W.a₃ = 0` (Fulton's `Y²Z = X³ + aX²Z + bXZ² + cZ³`,
  `a = a₂`, `b = a₄`, `c = a₆`), `Pᵢ = [xᵢ : yᵢ : 1]` are simple points with
  `P₁ ⊕ P₂ = P₃` in `CubicGroup W.polynomial O`, and either
  `x₁ ≠ x₂` and `λ = (y₁ − y₂)/(x₁ − x₂)`, or `P₁ = P₂` and
  `λ = (3x₁² + 2ax₁ + b)/(2y₁)`, then with `μ = y₁ − λx₁`,
  `x₃ = λ² − a − x₁ − x₂` and `y₃ = −λx₃ − μ`. Proof: transport by
  `toCubicGroup`, then Mathlib's `add_some`, `slope`, `addX`, `addY`
  with `a₁ = a₃ = 0`. No characteristic hypothesis is needed: in
  characteristic 2 with `a₁ = a₃ = 0` every simple point has `2P = O`, so
  the doubling case has no affine `P₃` and is vacuous. This is more general
  than the atlas's `char k ≠ 2`. The hypothesis `y₁ ≠ 0` of the doubling case
  is dropped too: if `2P₁` is an affine point `P₃`, then `y₁ ≠ −y₁`, so `y₁ ≠ 0`.
- **Problem 5.40**: for a subfield, given as `[Algebra k₀ k]` with
  `W₀ : WeierstrassCurve k₀` and `W = W₀.baseChange k`:
  **`rationalPoints k₀ W₀ : AddSubgroup (CubicGroup W.polynomial O)`**,
  the image of Mathlib's `Affine.Point.map (Algebra.ofId k₀ k)` under
  `toAffineAddEquiv.symm` and `toCubicGroup`, with
  `mem_rationalPoints_iff : P ∈ rationalPoints k₀ W₀ ↔
  ∃ v : Fin 3 → k₀, ∃ hv, P.point = mk k (algebraMap k₀ k ∘ v) hv`
  (the `k₀`-rational points form a subgroup), and
  `chord_mem_rationalPoints` (`φ` of two `k₀`-rational points is
  `k₀`-rational, as `φ(P, Q) = ⊖(P ⊕ Q)`; stated with N6's `CubicGroup.chord`,
  whose point is `thirdPoint`).

### Piece 5: cubics with a flex (`[IsAlgClosed k] [DecidableEq k]`)

Namespace `PlaneCurves.Projective`. This is the use of the bridge that the
atlas's formalization notes ask for, and the reason `DESIGN.md` lists N5
among N7's needs; N8 (torsion of the group of a nonsingular cubic) and D11
use it.

- `thirdPoint_linearSubst`: for invertible `M`, the projective map of `M`
  carries `thirdPoint (linearSubst M F)` to `thirdPoint F`, and
  `thirdPoint_smul` (`c ≠ 0`, already in N6): both from the uniqueness statement of N6
  and the invariance of intersection cycles under `linearSubst` and
  associates. These are lemmas about N6's definition and go in N6's
  `Cubic/Chord.lean`; if N1 lacks `intersectionCycle_linearSubst` (from
  Q6's invariance of intersection numbers), it goes in
  `Projective/IntersectionCycle.lean`.
- `exists_addEquiv_weierstrass_of_isFlex (hF : Irreducible F)
  (hd : F.IsHomogeneous 3) (hO : IsFlex F O) :
  ∃ (W : WeierstrassCurve k), Nonempty (CubicGroup F O ≃+ W.toProjective.Point)`,
  with `W.Δ ≠ 0` when `F` is nonsingular. Proof: N5 (c) gives `T`, `c`,
  `W`; the map of `T` is an additive equivalence between the two chord–tangent
  groups (by `thirdPoint_linearSubst`, `thirdPoint_smul`, and
  `T[0:1:0] = O`), and `toCubicGroup` finishes.

## The milestone

The milestone theorem is in `Cubic/Weierstrass.lean`, assembled from the
named results above, with its docstring ending in "Milestone: *Chord–tangent
addition on a Weierstrass cubic agrees with Mathlib's group law*."

`WeierstrassCurve.Projective.chordTangentAddition_eq_add`: for
`[IsAlgClosed k]` and `W : WeierstrassCurve.Projective k` (no `DecidableEq k`),
the conjunction of

1. `Irreducible W.polynomial`;
2. `IsSimplePoint W.polynomial O`, `IsFlex W.polynomial O` and
   `tangentLine W.polynomial ![0, 1, 0] = X 2`;
3. `IsNonsingular W.polynomial ↔ W.Δ ≠ 0`;
4. `∀ P, IsSimplePoint W.polynomial P ↔ P ∈ Set.range Point.toProjectivization`;
5. `∀ P Q : W.Point, thirdPoint W.polynomial P.toProjectivization Q.toProjectivization = (-(P + Q)).toProjectivization`;
6. `∀ P Q : W.Point, toCubicGroup (P + Q) = toCubicGroup P + toCubicGroup Q`
   (Fulton's `P ⊕ Q` with base point `O` is Mathlib's `P + Q`);
7. Problem 5.38, as in piece 4;
8. Problem 5.40: for `[Algebra k₀ k]`, `W₀` and `W = W₀.baseChange k`, the
   `k₀`-rational simple points form an `AddSubgroup` of
   `CubicGroup W.polynomial O`, and `φ` of two of them is one of them. Since
   `W` is fixed, this is stated for `W₀` with `W₀.baseChange k = W` as the
   existence of such a subgroup, which is `rationalPoints k₀ W₀`.

Parts 7 and 8 are stated inside the conjunction as the `∀` statements of
piece 4, so that the one theorem has the atlas's whole conclusion. The
statement is the atlas's, with `k` algebraically closed; parts 1, 2
(except the flex), 3 and 4 hold over any (perfect, for 3) field and are
stated so in their own lemmas.

## Notes

- **No second group law.** Associativity is not reproved for Weierstrass
  cubics: the `AddEquiv` is built from the bijection of piece 1 and the
  identity `P ⊕ Q = P + Q`, which uses only the definitions of `φ` and `⊕`
  and the line computations. The group axioms on both sides come from N6
  and Mathlib.
- **Projective, not affine, points.** The bridge starts from Mathlib's
  projective `W.Point`, so that Tau Ceti's `toProjectivization` applies at
  once; the affine group enters only through Mathlib's `toAffineAddEquiv`,
  inside proofs.
- **Problem 5.40 in the book's form** (any nonsingular cubic over `k₀`, any
  base point, possibly not a flex) is not part of the milestone, whose
  statement is for Weierstrass cubics. It is not planned here; the atlas
  sketches a direct proof from Problem 3.21 should a later part need it.
- **Characteristic 2.** Piece 5 needs a flex. When `char k ≠ 2` every
  nonsingular cubic has one (Q17); in characteristic 2 no milestone of the
  atlas provides one, so piece 5 keeps `IsFlex F O` as a hypothesis.
